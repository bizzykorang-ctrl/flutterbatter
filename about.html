Our story

Breakfast, boxed like a gift.

Flutter Batter started with a simple observation: in Accra, the best mornings are the ones someone else thought about for you. A warm box of breakfast, left on a desk, handed over at a gate, carried to a lecture hall — it says someone was thinking of you before the sun came up.

So we built a kitchen around that feeling. Every box is cooked fresh in the early hours, packed warm, and — if it's a gift — wrapped with ribbon and a note in your own words. No cold factory food, no sad plastic bags. Just a proper morning, boxed to be given.

We deliver across Greater Accra, Monday to Saturday, from first light until 11am. Order by 10:30am and your box travels the same morning.

— The Flutter Batter kitchen

What we insist on

Warm, not reheated

Morning means morning
