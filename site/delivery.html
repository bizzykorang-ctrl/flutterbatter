Zones & fees

How it works

  • Cut-off: order by 10:30am for same-morning delivery. Later orders roll to the next morning.
  • Timing: your zone's estimate window is shown at checkout. Riders text/WhatsApp when they're close.
  • Fees: charged per order by zone (not per box) — sending three boxes to one address costs one fee.
  • Tracking: every order gets a tracking number; follow it on the Track Order page.
  • Gifting: we can hand the box to the recipient directly — add their details and your note at checkout.
