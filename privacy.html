Privacy policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

What we collect

To deliver your box we need: your name, phone number, delivery address details, and the note you write. If you pay online, Paystack processes the payment and shares the payment reference and your email with us for receipts. If you create an account, we store your email, name and saved addresses.

How we use it

  • Preparing, packing and delivering your order.
  • WhatsApp/SMS updates about your order.
  • Receipts and payment confirmation.
  • Improving the menu (aggregated, anonymous statistics).

If you opt in to notifications, we may occasionally send menu announcements. You can opt out at any time from your browser or by messaging us.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • Share your address with anyone other than the rider delivering to you.
  • Store your card details — payments are handled entirely by Paystack (PCI-DSS compliant).

Data retention & your rights

Order records are kept for accounting (Ghana Revenue Authority requires 6 years). You can request a copy or deletion of your account data at hello@flutterbatter.com and we will action it within 30 days, minus what the law requires us to keep.
