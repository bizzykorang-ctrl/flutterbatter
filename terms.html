Terms of service

Last updated 18 September 2026

Orders

An order is confirmed when you receive an order number. Orders placed after the 10:30am cut-off are scheduled for the next delivery morning. We may refuse or refund an order if we can't meet our quality standard (for example, if a rider can't reach your area that morning) — and we'll tell you honestly and quickly.

Prices & payment

Prices are in Ghana Cedis and include packaging. Delivery fees are per order, by zone, shown at checkout. Online payments are processed by Paystack (card, MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash). Cash on delivery is available — please have the exact amount ready where possible.

Coupons

Coupons are single-use per customer unless stated, hold no cash value, and may carry minimum-order values or expiry dates. We may withdraw a coupon that was issued in error.

Delivery

We deliver to the address and instructions you provide. If nobody receives the box at delivery, the rider will wait 5 minutes and attempt a call/WhatsApp. Re-delivery may attract the zone fee again. Perishable food cannot be stored, so uncollected orders are unfortunately non-refundable after the delivery window.

Cancellations & refunds

  • Cancel free of charge any time before your box enters preparation (usually within minutes of ordering) — via WhatsApp or the track page.
  • Online refunds are returned via Paystack to the original payment method within 5–10 working days.

Food & allergens

Our kitchen handles eggs, wheat, milk, nuts and sesame. Boxes are prepared in a shared kitchen — if you have a severe allergy, please message us before ordering.

Liability

Our liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for it. Nothing in these terms excludes liability we cannot exclude under Ghanaian law.
